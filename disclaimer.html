Food safety and allergies

Follow safe food handling practices, keep perishable food cold, take care when opening spiky durian husks, cut fruit into small pieces for young children and store leftovers properly. Many recipes contain common allergens such as sesame, dairy, egg and wheat. Take care with boiling water and hot pans. We are not responsible for injury, illness or damage arising from cooking.
