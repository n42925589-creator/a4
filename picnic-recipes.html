Durian & tropical picnic recipes

Four easy, portable recipes for outdoor eating, from durian sticky rice cups to refreshing fruit skewers.

Vegan

Durian coconut sticky rice cups

Serves 61 hr (plus soaking)

Individual cups of sweet coconut sticky rice topped with durian are easy to carry and share outdoors.

Ingredients

  • 300 g glutinous rice, soaked for 4 hours
  • 400 ml can coconut milk
  • 50 g sugar and a pinch of salt
  • 200 g durian pods, seeds removed
  • Toasted sesame seeds

Method

  1. Steam the drained rice for 25–30 minutes until tender.
  2. Warm the coconut milk with the sugar and salt, then stir 300 ml into the hot rice and rest for 20 minutes.
  3. Spoon into lidded cups, top with durian and a drizzle of the remaining coconut milk.
  4. Sprinkle with sesame and keep in a cool bag until serving.

Picnic tip: Contains sesame. Eat within 4 hours of making and keep cool.

Vegetarian; contains wheat, egg and dairy

Durian cream pancake rolls

Makes 840 min

Soft pandan-tinted pancakes wrapped around whipped cream and durian, a favourite sweet treat across Southeast Asia.

Ingredients

  • 120 g plain flour and 2 eggs
  • 250 ml milk and 1 tbsp sugar
  • A few drops of pandan extract
  • 200 ml whipping cream, softly whipped
  • 200 g durian pods, mashed

Method

  1. Whisk the flour, eggs, milk, sugar and pandan into a smooth batter and rest for 15 minutes.
  2. Cook thin pancakes in a lightly oiled pan and leave to cool.
  3. Fold the durian into the cream.
  4. Place a spoonful on each pancake, fold into parcels and chill until firm.

Picnic tip: Pack in a cold cooler with ice packs and eat within 2 hours of leaving the fridge.

Vegan option

Durian banana smoothie

Serves 25 min

A thick, creamy smoothie that tames durian’s richness with banana and cold coconut drink.

Ingredients

  • 100 g frozen durian pods
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 300 ml coconut drink or milk of your choice
  • A handful of ice

Method

  1. Blend everything until smooth.
  2. Pour into a chilled flask and shake before drinking.

Picnic tip: Freeze durian pods in advance so the smoothie stays cold for longer.

Vegan

Tropical fruit skewers with lime salt

Serves 620 min

Bright, juicy skewers balance a rich durian dessert. A pinch of lime salt makes the flavours pop.

Ingredients

  • 300 g pineapple chunks
  • 300 g watermelon cubes
  • 2 mangoes, cubed
  • Zest of 2 limes mixed with 1 tsp flaky salt and a pinch of chilli
  • 12 skewers

Method

  1. Thread the fruit onto skewers.
  2. Pack in a lidded box on ice.
  3. Sprinkle with lime salt just before eating.

Picnic tip: Leave out the chilli for young children.

Packing tips

  • Chill everything well before you leave.
  • Pack the cooler with ice packs on top and bottom.
  • Keep drinks in a separate cooler so the food one stays shut.

Allergens

  • Some recipes contain wheat, egg, dairy or sesame.
  • Label boxes so guests can check ingredients.
  • Use separate utensils for allergy-friendly dishes.
