About Durian Picnic

An independent home-cooking site about durian, tropical fruit and relaxed outdoor eating.

Our story

A fruit worth getting to know

Durian divides opinion like no other fruit. We think it deserves a fair, friendly introduction: how to choose it, how to open it and how to enjoy it without upsetting the neighbours.

Durian Picnic pairs that guidance with simple, portable recipes and practical tools for planning meals outdoors.

Friendly

Clear guidance for first-timers and fans alike.

Tested

Recipes are made in ordinary home kitchens before publishing.

Safe

We follow recognised food safety guidance.

Independent

We sell nothing and aren’t tied to any brand.

Our tools, the variety explorer, the picnic planner and the food safety timer, offer general guidance only. Read more in our Editorial Policy.
